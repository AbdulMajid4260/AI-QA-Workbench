# Meridian Travel — synthetic booking policy
Version 1.0. This fictional policy supports document-grounded QA practice.

## BOOK-01
A traveler may reserve between one and six seats per booking. Zero seats or more than six seats must be rejected. A booking reference is created only after successful confirmation. Names used in QA are synthetic.

## CANCEL-01
Cancellation at least 48 hours before departure qualifies for a full refund of the base fare. Cancellation less than 48 hours before departure incurs a 20% base-fare deduction. No refund is due after departure. Processing fees are not specified.

## SUPPORT-01
Support operates 09:00–18:00 Asia/Kolkata, Monday through Saturday. Complaints must receive an initial acknowledgement within one working day. The policy does not define an international emergency number.
