NORTHSTAR JOURNEYS — SYNTHETIC TRAINING POLICY

This fictional document is for software testing practice only.

BK-01 — Seat quantity
A booking accepts between 1 and 6 seats inclusive.
A request for 0 seats must be rejected.
A request for 7 seats must be rejected.
A booking for 6 seats must be accepted when six seats are available.
Rejecting an invalid quantity must not create a booking reference.

CN-01 — Cancellation
Cancellation at least 48 hours before departure returns 100 percent of the base fare.
Cancellation less than 48 hours before departure, but before departure time, returns 75 percent of the base fare.
At departure time or later, there is no refund.
Approved refunds are processed within 5 working days.
Taxes and booking fees are outside this policy.

Unspecified information
The policy does not provide a customer helpline number or baggage allowance.
Use synthetic traveler names and booking identifiers only.
No live booking application is supplied with this document.
